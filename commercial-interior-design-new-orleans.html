Commercial interior design in New Orleans

Flex Design Studio is a New Orleans architecture and interiors firm that designs offices, restaurants and bars, and retail spaces for commercial clients in the city and across the country. We are based in the Bywater and work from the first sketch through construction administration, with every project modeled in BIM from schematic planning onward so that what you approve is what gets built.

What we design

Our commercial interior design work covers the spaces where businesses meet their people and their customers:

  • Workplace and office design for headquarters, expansions, laboratories and tenant improvements
  • Hospitality design for bars, restaurants, nightclubs, music venues and rooftop lounges
  • Retail design for storefronts, studios and showrooms
  • Cannabis dispensary design for retail and cultivation and manufacturing facilities nationwide

Why a local studio matters in New Orleans

Commercial buildings in New Orleans are often older, with existing conditions that rarely match the drawings, and they sit in a climate that is hard on materials. A studio that works here regularly knows how to survey those conditions, how to detail for humidity and heavy use, and how local permitting and contractors tend to work. We walk your site, verify what is really there, and design around it instead of discovering it during construction.

How a project runs

Every project follows the same four phases, scaled to the size of the space:

  • Schematic planning: programming and early layout options grounded in your goals and the site
  • Design development: materials, finishes, lighting and furniture direction, with renderings and fly-throughs
  • Construction documentation: drawings accurate enough for contractors to bid and build from with confidence
  • Construction administration: on-site coordination through completion, keeping the build aligned to the design

We also coordinate with engineering, security and AV and IT consultants, and with local permitting bodies, so the project moves through approvals without surprises.

See the space before it is built

BIM keeps architecture, finishes and consultant coordination in one model. Photorealistic renderings let you decide on finishes, lighting and materials before anything is ordered, and animated fly-throughs support approvals, leasing and investor presentations.

Frequently asked questions

What does a commercial interior designer do?

A commercial interior designer plans and designs interior spaces for businesses: the layout, finishes, lighting and furniture, and the coordination with engineers, consultants and contractors that carries the design through construction.

Yes. Flex Design Studio works nationally, with projects in Illinois, Missouri, New York, New Jersey and California as well as Louisiana.

We coordinate with engineering, security and AV and IT consultants and with local permitting bodies, and we prepare drawings accurate enough for permitting and for contractor bidding.
